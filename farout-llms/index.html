Editorial policy

What earns a place?

The unit of coverage is the mechanism, not the checkpoint. An entry must change the computation graph, the memory interface, the representation, the arithmetic, or the way the model generates language.

Include means a distinct mechanism with credible language-model evidence. Watch means the idea is materially different but evidence is still narrow or preliminary. Routine scaling, standard expert routing, post-training quantization, and serving-only changes are out of scope.

Diagrams are original, simplified schematics. Parameter and context figures refer to the named experiment or release, and author-reported results are not independent replications. Dates refer to initial papers or technical announcements unless noted.
